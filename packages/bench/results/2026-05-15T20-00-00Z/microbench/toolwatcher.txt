ToolWatcher CPU microbench — v0.4.1 rerun on lab (gcc:13 container build, AMD EPYC 8124P)

Command: bench_watcher 1000000 0.05 1024
  (1M synthetic tokens, 5% region density, 1024-id chunks)

Result:
  tokens             1000000
  region_density     0.050  (regions seen: 4030)
  passthrough ids    943580
  detok chars        3541540

  watcher (codec_tool_watcher_feed)  2.08 ns/token   481.09 Mtok/s   total 2.08 ms
  detokenize (codec_detokenizer_render) 55.42 ns/token   18.04 Mtok/s   total 55.42 ms
  speedup            26.7×

Note: differs from pre-v0.4.1 README claim (0.61 ns/token, 1648 Mtok/s, ~100×).
The earlier number was captured on a different CPU/compiler combination
(not documented in the older capture). Today's number is honest on the lab's
AMD EPYC 8124P; the speedup ratio (26.7×) is what matters for the marketing
claim and is still strongly in ToolWatcher's favour.
